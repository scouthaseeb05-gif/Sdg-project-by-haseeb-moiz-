The goals are universal. They apply to every country and recognise that ending poverty must go together with protecting the planet. My work focuses most on SDG 13 (Climate Action), with SDG 3, 11, 12 and 15 close behind.

History of the SDGs

  • 1992 · Earth Summit, Rio de Janeiro. Countries agree on Agenda 21, a first global plan for sustainable development.
  • 2000 · Millennium Development Goals. Eight goals aim to cut extreme poverty and improve health and education by 2015.
  • 2012 · Rio+20. World leaders agree to create a new set of universal goals.
  • 2015 · Agenda 2030. The UN adopts the 17 Sustainable Development Goals.
  • Today · Countdown to 2030. Governments, schools, scouts and citizens report on progress each year.

Methodology for sustainable development

This is the method I follow in every project.

  1. Identify. Spot a local problem and match it to an SDG.
  2. Plan. Set a target, a team, materials and a timeline.
  3. Act. Run the activity with the patrol and the community.
  4. Measure. Record people reached, waste cleared, trees planted.
  5. Report and follow up. Write the report and keep looking after the result.

Piled-up garbage at Jinnah Ground 90 caused a bad smell and mosquito breeding, a risk to the children and players who use it. I led the idea, planning and coordination. Scouts, Girl Guides and local citizens cleaned the ground, separated waste, removed broken glass safely with sheets and gloves, and planted 2–3 trees each. Supervisor: Hasan Kirmani (SL).

Community participation in the plantation

Result: cleaner, safer ground, smell removed, mosquito sources reduced, and new greenery. The team will keep visiting to look after the trees.

Ecosip · Climate Action (Raven Patrol)

Scout Bazaar Season 04 · Karimabad Jamaat Khana
43121315

Ecosip is a community plastic waste reduction initiative. Only about 20% of plastic bottles in our community are recycled, so Raven Patrol set out to replace plastic bottles with biodegradable ones and collect bottles for recycling.

  • Awareness: sessions at community centers and schools, plus videos shared on the unit's Facebook page.
  • Collection drives: people brought plastic bottles and entered a lucky draw. Bottles went to recycling factories.
  • Wall of Help: 200 pairs of clothes, including school uniforms, collected for reuse.
  • Trash into Treasure (25 Dec 2024) and Eco Plastic projects on the SDG platform: turning waste into something useful.

Challenges: logistics of bottle collection and transport, and the effort of promoting biodegradable bottles. Next: more schools and events, and partnering with local businesses to replace single-use plastics.

Made by Haseeb · Raven Patrol

Scout Bazaar 2022 · Good Health and Well-Being

2022 · Visit to Dar-ul-Sukoon old age home
3

Scouts spent a morning at Dar-ul-Sukoon, which cares for elderly and special individuals. We talked with residents, shared breakfast, painted nails, played games and ran a painting session with paper and colors. A visitor played guitar and sang, since no sound system was available.

  • Emotional well-being and social inclusion were the focus, not only material gifts.
  • Scouts learned empathy, patience and teamwork.
  • Lesson: care for our elders as they cared for us.

Carbon Calculator

A personal carbon footprint tracker. Log electricity, gas, vehicle travel, flights and trees planted. It turns each entry into kg of CO₂, shows total emissions, offsets and net emissions with charts, and gives AI sustainability insights. Built in September 2026.

Formula: CO₂ (kg) = Amount × Emission Factor, using EPA and DEFRA 2024 factors. Trees planted count as negative emissions. Supports SDG 13, 12, 7 and 15.
